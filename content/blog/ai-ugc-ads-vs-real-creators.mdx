## Why brands run out of creative

Paid social needs more than one good ad. A creator shoot can look great and still leave you with a handful of hooks, a long wait and a bill that arrives before you know what converts.

AI UGC is creator-style video made with AI creators, voices and scenes instead of hired actors. It is advertising, not a customer review. The point is to test more angles without booking a studio.

## What changes when the creative is AI

You still need a brief. We script for natural speech, match the creator to the audience, and edit with captions, pacing and sound so the ad fits the feed. You approve the brief and the scripts before production.

On the Monthly UGC Pack, starting from $1,500 a month, a brand gets 8–10 videos with 3 hook variations each. Most videos take 5–10 business days from an approved script. That is the practical difference: more versions in the account, on a schedule, without shipping product to an actor.

## What stays the same

The offer, the targeting and the spend still decide the result. A sharp hook cannot rescue a weak product page. You or your media buyer launch the ads. We make the creative.

## Key takeaways

- AI UGC is a production method, not a fake testimonial.
- Volume matters because paid social burns through hooks.
- You approve the brief before anything is generated.
- Ad performance still depends on the offer, the audience and the spend.
